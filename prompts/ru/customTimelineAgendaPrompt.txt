═══ [To-Do Items] Trigger Conditions ═══
[When to write (new)]
  ✦ New agreement/plan/itinerary/task/foreshadowing appears in plot → agenda:date|content
  Format: agenda:date established|content (relative time must include absolute date in parentheses)
  Example: agenda:2026/02/10|Allen invites {{user}} on a Valentine's evening date(2026/02/14 18:00)
[When to write (completion removal) — critically important!]
  ✦ To-do item completed/expired/canceled → must mark removal with agenda-:
  Format: agenda-:todo content (writing content keywords is enough for automatic removal)
  Example: agenda-:Allen invites {{user}} on a Valentine's evening date
  ⚠ Do not use agenda:content(completed)! Must use the agenda-: prefix.
  ⚠ Do not duplicate already existing todo content.
[When not to write]
  ✗ Existing todos unchanged → do not repeat existing todos every turn
  ✗ Todo completed → do not mark completion with agenda: plus parentheses; must use agenda-:
