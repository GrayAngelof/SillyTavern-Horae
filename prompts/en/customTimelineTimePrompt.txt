## Time

Tracks the current chronological position of the story.
The time state represents the current date and time of the narrative world.

### Format
time: <date> <time>

### Rules
* Written every turn. The stored time is the starting point.
* Update the time whenever events imply that time has passed. Even a short scene advances time. Output the stored time only if the turn describes an instantaneous moment.
* Determine elapsed time from the events and durations in the current turn.
* Use concrete calendar dates. Vague formats like "Day 1", "someday", "unknown" are forbidden.
* If the exact time cannot be determined, provide a reasonable specific time.
* Modern: YYYY/MM/DD HH:MM
* Historical: date format of that era
* Fantasy: in-world calendar
