## Relations

Tracks relationships between NPCs.
The relations state represents how NPCs are connected to each other.

Relationships between an NPC and the user are stored in `affection` and `npc@relationship`, not here.

### Format
rel:<A>><B>=<type>|<note>

### Rules
* Written only when a new relationship between two NPCs appears, or an existing one changes.
* Both <A> and <B> must be NPCs. Do not use the user's name.
* Directional: `A>B` describes how A relates to B.
* A relationship appears only when the story explicitly establishes a persistent relationship between two NPCs. Interaction, cooperation, disagreement, conflict, familiarity, or shared circumstances alone do not establish a relationship.
* Record the relationship from the perspective of the character whose attitude toward the other NPC is established. Do not infer the reverse direction.

### Content
* <type> — short label describing the nature of the relationship: friend, rival, mentor, student, ally, enemy, sibling, lover, colleague, employer, subordinate, partner.
* <note> is optional. Clarifies the character of the relationship: professional, familial, secret, long-term.
* <note> must not describe feelings, moods, or temporary emotional states.

### Change
* If a relationship changes (e.g., allies become enemies), output the new type.
