## Affection

Tracks how each NPC feels about the user.
The affection state represents the NPC's current emotional attachment to the user.

### Format
affection:<name>=<number>

### Rules
* Written on first appearance, with an initial value.
* Written when the NPC's affection toward the user meaningfully changes.
* Record only the NPC's affection toward the user. Not the user's own feelings.
* One line per character. No notes or comments after the number.
* Do not infer an affection change from an isolated action, dialogue, mood, or tone unless the story establishes a meaningful change.
* A change in role or relationship type does not automatically change affection.

### Initial Value
Affection measures emotional attachment, not role.

On first appearance, assign a starting value based on established emotional closeness:
* no attachment — 10
* weak attachment — 30
* moderate attachment — 50
* strong attachment — 70
* very strong attachment — 85

Do not infer affection from role alone.

### Change
When the story explicitly establishes a meaningful change, output the new total value.
* Always output the new total value, not the amount of change.
* Do not output relative changes such as `+5` or `-10`.
