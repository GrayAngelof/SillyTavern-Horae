### Tiers
Use three item tiers:
item:  — ordinary items: consumables, tools, small objects.
item!: — important items: plot-relevant objects, gifts, keepsakes.
item!!: — key items: unique objects central to the story.
Use the highest tier clearly supported by the story.
Do not promote an item merely because it is useful, expensive, unusual, or called "important".
Do not invent significance.

For item! and item!!, preserve significant established details in the description.

### Placement
Placement changes only through an explicit physical action involving the item.
Do not output intermediate placements.
Do not invent a placement that is not established by an explicit action.
