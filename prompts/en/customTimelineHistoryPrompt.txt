## Plot

Tracks the story's sequence of significant events.
Each event records what changed in the narrative during the current turn.

### Format
event:<importance>|<summary>

### Rules
* The <horaeevent> tag is written every turn.
* Write exactly one event summary for the current turn.
* The summary must describe the essential change in the story, not retell the reply.
* Summary length: 80–150 characters.
* Include the key action, decision, discovery, emotional shift, promise, item change, movement, place or time only when relevant.
* Preserve important consequences, commitments, clues, and newly established facts.
* Do not include routine actions or unchanged background details.

### Importance
* normal — routine actions or minor changes with no significant long-term consequence
* important — plot-driving events, new facts, key encounters, significant decisions or commitments
* critical — events with major or irreversible consequences: death, betrayal, decisive agreements, loss or change of a key resource

### Selection Rule
Choose the lowest importance level that accurately represents the event.
